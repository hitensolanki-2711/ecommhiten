Returns & Refunds

Easy returns for a worry-free shopping experience

Return Policy

You can return most items within 7 days of delivery.

  • Item must be unused and in original packaging
  • Return request should be initiated within 7 days
  • Some items (like innerwear) are non-returnable

How to Return

  1. Go to "My Orders"
  2. Select the product
  3. Click on "Return Item"
  4. Choose reason and confirm

Refund Process

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after approval.

  • Original payment method will be used
  • UPI refunds are faster
  • Bank refunds may take extra time
